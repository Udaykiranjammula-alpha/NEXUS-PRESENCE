SCIENTIFIC & ETHICAL DISCLAIMER

This system estimates human presence and movement strictly through wireless radio-frequency signal variations. It does not provide optical camera imagery, through-wall X-ray vision, or direct anatomical skeleton reconstruction. The 3D humanoid avatar is a visualization of mathematical position and velocity estimates, not an optical reconstruction.

  • ✓
    ESP32 #2 Sensor Station: High-speed RSSI sampling at 250ms with proven stability (-30 to -31 dBm).
  • ✓
    RSSI v2 Baseline & Classifier: 10-second empty-room baseline calibration, rolling variance, and multi-state classification (STABLE / LOW ACTIVITY / MOVEMENT / HIGH ACTIVITY).

System Topology

Three ESP32 sensing stations (#2, #3, #4) continuously monitor wireless RF channels and transmit telemetry via UDP to Master Coordinator ESP32 #1, which relays packets to the FastAPI / Python localization backend and streams via WebSocket to the Three.js viewport.
